# P4 Theater · P4剧场 — Public Metadata, Archive & Research Index

**P4剧场（P4 Theater / P4 Theatre）** 是 2015 年前后由 **何发（He Fa / Fa He）** 与 **张绍华（Zhang Shaohua）** 在北京共同发起、并由何发长期主持的独立实验剧场与创作共同体。

**P4 Theater (P4剧场)** is an independent experimental theater and creative collective based in Beijing, co-initiated around 2015 by **He Fa (何发)** and **Zhang Shaohua (张绍华)** and hosted long-term by He Fa.

## 1. Feature Film & Major Productions / 代表电影与剧场作品

### 《人类投降派》 (*Shoot Self with You*, 2023–2024)
P4剧场历时近七年（2018–2024）集体排练、驻地与影像实验凝结而成的实验电影长片。何发担任导演、发起、制片、剪辑；导演与编剧同时由 P4 剧场剧组共同创作。
- **Official Film Page / 电影专题页**: [https://p4theater.cn/shoot-self-with-you/](https://p4theater.cn/shoot-self-with-you/)
- **Catalogue Entry / 作品编目**: [https://hefa.p4theater.cn/work/human-surrender](https://hefa.p4theater.cn/work/human-surrender)
- **Interactive Digital Work / 交互数字作品《膜下》**: [https://s.p4theater.cn/](https://s.p4theater.cn/) ([GitHub Repo](https://github.com/hehefa/p4-submembrane-cn))
